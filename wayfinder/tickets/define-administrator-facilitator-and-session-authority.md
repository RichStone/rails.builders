# Define Administrator and Facilitator Authority

- Label: `wayfinder:grilling`

## Question

Which actions require the assignable Administrator role, what the Facilitator flag means beyond public presentation, how multiple Facilitators are presented publicly, and what safeguards prevent loss of the last Administrator?

## Settled input

Facilitators are operational notification recipients, not only public presenters. Every Facilitator must receive email when a verified person enters the enrollment lifecycle, including transitions to the waitlist, a Seat Offer, Active Builder, declined, expired, withdrawn, left waitlist, or removed. A person who holds both Facilitator and Administrator roles must receive only one copy. Whether Administrators who are not Facilitators also receive these messages remains to be settled.
