# Resolution: Define Administrator and Facilitator Authority

Members may manage their own profile, Products, publication request, account, and enrollment choices. They cannot grant themselves a Seat, change queue order, assign roles, or retry external integrations.

Administrators manage the program, capacity, enrollment and queue exceptions, Builder eligibility, roles, accounts, and integration failures. The application must always retain at least one verified Administrator: the last Administrator cannot be demoted or deleted, including through self-service account deletion.

Facilitator and Administrator are independent roles. Facilitators appear in the public facilitator section, review profile-publication requests, and do not consume Seats. Multiple Facilitators are allowed. A Facilitator has no Administrator authority unless assigned both roles. Authority over live sessions belongs to the next session-experience phase rather than v1 membership administration.
