# Resolution: Newsletter Consent Requirements

Resolved by [the cited research](../research/newsletter-consent.md): email verification alone does not authorize adding a Registrant to the Loop Labs newsletter, and privacy-policy disclosure alone is not consent. Subscribe only after a separate, optional, affirmative newsletter choice and newsletter-specific confirmation; retain reproducible evidence and provide an equally easy withdrawal path. Counsel must confirm the controller identity, any § 7(3) UWG customer exception, final copy, transfers/tracking, and retention periods.
