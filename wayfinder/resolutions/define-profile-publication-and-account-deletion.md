# Resolution: Define Profile Publication and Account Deletion

The current publication workflow is final: a Builder explicitly requests publication, a Facilitator approves it, and any later profile or Product change clears approval. Email is never public. Turning publication off removes the public card immediately and does not change enrollment, Slack Entitlement, or the private account.

Deleting the account immediately removes the public profile, Products, image, enrollment, and live local access, and releases any Seat or offer. It also creates or supersedes a durable manual Slack offboarding task, so external cleanup does not block deletion. Only the minimum verified email, Slack identifiers, desired state, and audit timestamps needed to complete deactivation may outlive the account; they are removed or reduced to a non-personal operational audit record after an Owner/Admin verifies completion. A later re-registration creates a new current admission decision and must visibly supersede any stale task before an Administrator acts.

The separately confirmed Loop Labs newsletter remains a separate relationship: deleting Rails Builders does not silently unsubscribe or delete the ClickFunnels contact.
